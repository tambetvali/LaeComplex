# Laegna Complex / Two Body Problems

Topology of two bodies:
- One body has center, and grows out from it.
- The other body has the same.
- Bodies are related.

Let's assume Laegna number topology:
- First body is at plus/minus 180 degrees, centered at zero.
- Second body is at plus/minus *second span of* 180 degrees, centered at zero.

This makes it typical 0 degree to 720 degree problem, compatible to hilbert's spaces and laegna two-dimensional number models:
- Two forms
  - Simpler locally: Where one axe ends at 180, other starts from -180.
  - Simpler globally: Where one axe ends at 180, other starts from 180; where one ends from -180, other starts from -180;
    - Number space, then, must undergo some transformations: now, the moebius curvature is not global, but in local understanding.

Hilbert's int ext space can be used to understand this:
- Outer space collapses into singularity.
- If linear growth is double-inversed into linear collapse, the result is reverse-symmetric.

All relations appear:
- Space of one to body of other.
- Body of one to body of other.
- Space of one to space of other.

Typically, frequency is vertical - in Laegna, it's seen as horizontal, in-space frequency.

One can now do this:
- Apply transformation matrix on both bodies to make their relative directions and sizes match where they physically are dispositioned.
- Resulting matrix space is compatible to hilbert space with square dimensional density - both bodies have their dimensional density, one has one side 1 and other side infinity, other has vice versa, resulting numbers simplify all the combinations: trivialities, such as going one rank up or multiplying the numbers, reveal hidden combinations and free variable space - I think 1/2 to 2\1 relation as perfect free variable combinator was the actual *first* theorem behind what later became laegna base-4, not the qualities used to define base-4 on itself.
- When all relations are made:
  - Apply the transformation matrices on both the space and values *now in reverse*.
  - Both bodies are in their initial position, for example standing facing frontwards - yet the positions, altough not mapping exactly to this space but being kind of between-tesimals, yet losslessly roundable in their problem-solution space proximity if all conversions were laegna numbers with small information; the resulting math is able to symmetrically see the body disposition.

This can be extended to higher spaces - each new dimension / variable adds possibility to new bodies.

Class on these instances will be symbol or archetype of the body, it's appearance in real-world reflection is rather the metaphor.
- Because they are disproportionally interrelated in terms of inner relations, but topology is kept, the resulting shape is somewhat poetic - the fractal head topology.
